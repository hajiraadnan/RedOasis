## About the Project
Notable space agencies such as NASA and SpaceX are preparing to send more missions to Mars, with SpaceX planning uncrewed cargo missions by 2028 and NASA planning human missions by the late 2030s.

These missions require significant funding and large amounts of rocket fuel that would otherwise have to be carried to and from Mars. To solve this, our team created **RedOasis** around using Martian water-ice hydrogen reserves and atmospheric carbon dioxide to produce rocket fuel locally.

Using the **Sabatier reaction**, hydrogen extracted from Martian ice and carbon dioxide from the atmosphere are combined to synthesize methane for rocket propulsion. Our interactive map highlights the main areas where these raw materials are abundant and where methane synthesis outposts could be built. This reduces rocket launch mass and helps sustain self-sufficient energy production for future Martian colonies.
